# Erin Spencer

Independent researcher working at the intersection of mathematical visualization,
applied philosophy, and human–AI collaboration.

This GitHub hosts projects developed under the framework of *The Interdependent Way*,
with a focus on clarity, verifiability, and non-coercive use of intelligence systems.

---

## AI Council (Consultative, Non-Authoritative)

The following artificial intelligence systems are used as *independent consultative agents*.
No single system is treated as authoritative; congruence and dissent are explicitly preserved.

- ChatGPT (OpenAI) — synthesis, structured reasoning, formal drafting
- Grok (xAI) — adversarial critique, assumption testing
- Gemini (Google DeepMind) — cross-domain abstraction, alternative framing
- Perplexity — source-oriented recall and citation discovery
- DeepSeek — formal logic and technical compression

All conclusions, errors, and interpretations remain human-owned.

---

## Operating Principles

- AI is a tool, not an authority
- Dissent is preserved, not smoothed over
- Clarity outweighs persuasion
- Visual intuition must eventually reduce to formal structure

---

## Current Focus

- Prime distribution and modular visualizations
- Unit circle and cylindrical mappings of arithmetic structure
- Human–AI interface ethics (rights and responsibilities, bidirectional)
